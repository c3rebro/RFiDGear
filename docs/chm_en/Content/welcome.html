RFiDGear Help

RFiDGear is a Windows program for encoding and checking RFID chips: MIFARE Classic, MIFARE DESFire, MIFARE Ultralight and NTAG. You create a list of tasks, save it as a project and run it for a chip – once at your workstation, or automatically for every new card of a series. A report, filled into a PDF form, documents what was done to the chip.

Who this help is for

  • Part 1 – Working with RFiDGear: for everyone who operates RFiDGear at a workstation: setting up the reader, creating tasks, saving projects, encoding and checking chips, creating reports.
  • Part 2 – For developers: for everyone who drives RFiDGear from a program of their own (external control over a named pipe), supplies card keys from their own application, or writes an extension for RFiDGear.

This help does not explain how the individual RFID technologies work. The data sheets of the chip manufacturers are the right source for that. Here you find what RFiDGear does with them and how to operate it.

System requirements

  • Operating system: Windows 11, or Windows 10 in a version that .NET 10 supports (32 or 64 bit).
  • .NET 10 Desktop Runtime: the setup brings it along.
  • Reader: an Elatec TWN4 or a PC/SC compatible reader. You choose the reader in the settings; without a selected reader you cannot create chip-related tasks.
