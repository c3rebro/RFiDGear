What is proven and what is not

This page says honestly what you can rely on and what has so far run only in a test or not at all. It follows the notes in the source repository; where the files there are newer, they are right.

External control

  • The protocol is not frozen. It will be frozen when the checklist RFiDGear.HIL.Tests/EXTERNAL-CONTROL.md has passed with a real reader. Until then a name may still change; after it, nothing is removed or re-meant within major version 1, and a client reads what a newer RFiDGear adds as optional.
  • Tested against a simulated reader: the basic frame, the revision of the project, the origin of a request for a key and the mode with approval. The tests run the real interface on a real pipe, the real state machine of automatic mode and the real main window against a reader that plays a script. A real reader and a real card are needed to see how a real reader reports a card that is taken away between two polls, how long a real read takes against the time you allow, and what a real task does to a real card. The cases from 52 on in EXTERNAL-CONTROL.md are for that.
  • The sample client and its tests prove that the advice in this help works together with the interface; they do not prove that your client does it right.

What that means for your work

  • Do not write in series through external control until you have played the flow through by hand with your project on test cards (the sample client helps), and check the result of every card yourself: a positive end of the task list is not the same as a card that holds what was meant.
  • Build a client so that it copes with a name that may still change: read the capabilities, not the version.
  • What the help calls "not implemented" or "without function", do not use.
